Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you enroll.

Do we need any experience?

No. Every course begins with posture, pen hold and basic strokes, and each group is paced for its age.

Are supplies included?

Starter supplies (pens, nibs, ink and practice sheets) are used in class. A take-home kit is available for an extra fee.

Can my child try a class first?

Yes. We hold a free demo class every month; join the open-day list to get the next date.

Can fees be paid in parts?

Term fees can be paid in two instalments. Ask us when you enroll.

Do you teach left-handed students?

Yes. We adjust paper angle, pen choice and posture for left-handed writers.

What if we miss a class?

One missed class per term can be made up in another batch of the same course.

Prefer to talk to us?

Call during studio hours and a teacher will help you choose the right course.
