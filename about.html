About Us

Teaching calligraphy and handwriting with patience and care.

Our story

Inkwell began as weekend classes for a handful of children in Gopalapuram. Parents asked for adult evenings, then for teens, and the studio grew into eight courses and four age groups.

Every class still follows one idea: short demonstrations, long practice and kind, specific feedback.

Our journey

How the studio grew, year by year.

  1. 2012: First weekend class

    Six children, one table and a box of nibs.

  2. 2016: Adult evening courses

    Copperplate and brush lettering for adults.

  3. 2020: Teen programme

    Italic Studio and Gothic Blackletter added.

  4. 2025: Eight courses

    Wedding stationery and handwriting repair launched.
